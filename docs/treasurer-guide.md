# Treasurer Quick Guide – Bodax SACCO

> **Role**: The Treasurer manages all financial records for the SACCO. Your key responsibilities are recording savings, processing loans, and approving withdrawals.

---

## Task 1 – Record a Member's Savings Deposit

1. Log in with your Treasurer account.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Savings → Record Savings**.
3. Select the member from the **Member** dropdown (their current balance appears below it).
4. Enter the **Amount (UGX)** and confirm the **Date** of the deposit.
5. Add optional **Notes** if needed, then click **Record Savings**.
6. A success message confirms the deposit has been saved.

---

## Task 2 – Confirm a Member's Savings Receipt

1. In the sidebar, click **Savings → Confirm Deposits**.
2. Select the member from the dropdown.
3. Enter the deposited **Amount**, **Date**, and any **Notes**.
4. Click **Confirm deposit** to record and confirm the receipt.

---

## Task 3 – Review and Approve a Loan Request

1. In the sidebar, click **Loans → Confirm Loans**.
2. You will see a list of **Pending** loan requests.
3. Review the requested amount and member details.
4. Click **Approve** to move the request to approved status, or **Reject** to decline it.
5. To formally issue the loan after approval, click **Issue Loan** and fill in the Principal, Interest Rate, Installments, and Due Date.
6. Click **Issue Loan** to make the loan active.

---

## Task 4 – Record a Loan Repayment

1. In the sidebar, click **Loans → Treasurer Loans**.
2. Find the active loan and note the **Loan ID**.
3. In the repayment section, select the **Member**, enter the **Amount** paid and **Date**.
4. Click **Record Repayment** to update the outstanding balance.

---

## Task 5 – Approve or Reject a Withdrawal Request

1. In the sidebar, click **Withdrawals**.
2. Review pending withdrawal requests from members.
3. Click **Approve** to authorise the withdrawal and deduct it from their savings balance, or **Reject** to decline it.
4. A confirmation message appears on success.
