# Member Quick Guide – Bodax SACCO

> **Role**: As a Member, you can view your savings and loan activity, request loans, request savings withdrawals, and update your profile.

---

## Task 1 – View Your Dashboard & Savings Balance

1. Log in with your Member account (phone number or email + password).
2. You will be taken directly to your **Dashboard**.
3. Your current **Savings Balance**, recent deposits, and any active loans are displayed on this page.
4. Upcoming or overdue loan reminders appear at the bottom.

---

## Task 2 – Request a Loan

1. In the left sidebar, click **Loans**.
2. If you are eligible, an **Apply for Loan** form will appear. If not, the reason for ineligibility is displayed.
3. Fill in the **Amount**, **Purpose**, **Number of Installments**, and **Due Date**.
4. Click **Submit Request**.
5. Your request will appear under **My Loan Requests** with a status of *Pending* until the Treasurer reviews it.

---

## Task 3 – Request a Savings Withdrawal

1. In the sidebar, click **Withdraw**.
2. Enter the **Amount** you wish to withdraw and the **Reason** for the withdrawal.
3. Click **Submit Request**.
4. The request is sent to the Treasurer for approval. You will see the status update on your dashboard.

---

## Task 4 – View Your Statement & Loan History

1. In the sidebar, click **Statements**.
2. Select a **Start Date** and **End Date** to filter your savings history.
3. Click **Load Statement** to view a detailed list of all deposits in that period.
4. To view your loan history, click **Loans** in the sidebar and scroll down to see all active, completed, and pending requests.

---

*If you cannot log in or have a payment dispute, contact the Treasurer or refer to the [Pilot Support Checklist](./pilot-support.md).*
