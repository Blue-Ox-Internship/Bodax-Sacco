# Chairman Quick Guide – Bodax SACCO

> **Role**: As the Chairman, you have read-only oversight of all SACCO activity. You can view all dashboards, analytics reports, and export summaries.

---

## Task 1 – View the Chairman Dashboard

1. Log in with your Chairman account.
2. You are taken directly to the **Chairman Dashboard**.
3. Review the headline figures:
   - **Total Members**, **Total Savings**, **Active Loans**, **Outstanding Loan Balance**
   - **Loan Arrears**, **Weekly Collections**, and **Monthly Collections**
4. These numbers are calculated live from the database whenever you visit the page.

---

## Task 2 – Review Analytics (Top Savers, Defaulters, Trends)

1. In the left sidebar, click **Analytics**.
2. The page displays:
   - **Top Savers** – top 10 members by net savings balance.
   - **Defaulters** – members with overdue loan balances and days overdue.
   - **Monthly Collection Trend** – a 6-month bar chart of total savings collected.
   - **Income Summary** – total savings collected, loan repayments received, and interest earned.
   - **Expenditure Summary** – total withdrawals paid out.
3. Use this data to guide decisions in SACCO meetings.

---

## Task 3 – View and Export Reports

1. In the sidebar, click **Reports**.
2. Select the report type (e.g., Overdue Loans, Member Savings Summary).
3. Review the data in the table.
4. Use your browser's **Print** function (Ctrl+P) to save the report as a PDF for record-keeping.
